## App Description

### Promotional Text - Short Description (170 characters)
Master the ancient art of potion brewing. Mix ingredients, control temperature, and craft legendary elixirs in your medieval alchemist laboratory.

### Full Description

Potion Works is an offline alchemy crafting game with a four-phase brewing mini-game (mix, heat, stir, bottle). Discover 52 original hand-drawn ingredients across minerals, plants, liquids, and creatures; fulfill customer orders; unlock 14 recipes in the recipe book — all progress stored on device with no account required.
